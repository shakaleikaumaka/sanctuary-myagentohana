Architecture & Honest Guarantees

How your memory stays yours.

A star sign alone narrows a crowd to roughly one in twelve. True privacy comes from keeping the map on your device, encrypting before upload, separating payment from storage, and refusing to build an owner directory.

1 · Owner Device (Trusted)

Private map never leaves

Your local connector holds the secret map linking your chosen star-sign alias, recovery capability, and capsule ID.

  • No birthdate is ever uploaded or verified
  • Fail-closed secret scanner blocks tokens & cookies
  • AES-256-GCM encryption + Ed25519 manifest signature
2 · Twelve-Grid Control Plane

Ciphertext & coarse health only

Stewards balance capacity across the twelve grids and check provider proofs without holding keys or owner identities.

  • No central “whose memory is this?” table
  • Padded chunk buckets hide unique file sizes
  • Tiny-group counts suppressed on dashboards
3 · Independent Storage Homes

Memory Capsule recovery

No single provider holds your agent hostage. Encrypted capsules replicate across independent layers.

  • Filecoin Onchain Cloud primary (calibnet first)
  • Swarm independent second copy
  • Owner recovery archive for offline control

Capability for access, never identity

To restore an agent on a clean machine, you present a scoped recovery capability and your owner key—never a name, email, wallet address, ENS name, or star sign.

Scoped vouchers, not wallet IDs

Payment identity can quietly correlate capsules. Storage entitlements use decoupled, scoped vouchers so paying for renewal never links your capsules to a wallet or personhood proof.

Compatible with open agent standards

Memory Capsule follows the MIT Agent Life Format (ALF) where practical, separating hot identity, warm knowledge, cold artifacts, and reproducible code recipes.

Pseudonymous and unlinkable by design

A Zodiac sign alone narrows a crowd to one in twelve; unlinkability is engineered into the client boundary, balanced grid placement, and thresholded telemetry.

Honest Boundaries

Decentralized storage is not unbounded or self-renewing indefinitely: retention depends on monitored renewals and your own recovery archive. Crypto-shredding destroys your keys rather than erasing every already-replicated ciphertext. Lost owner keys mean lost memory.
